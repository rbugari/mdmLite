# MDM Lite Commercial Intro

## Short Positioning

MDM Lite is a lightweight governed rule store for teams that need to stop managing operational mappings, groups, and parameters in spreadsheets, scattered SQL, or embedded application code.

## What Problem It Solves

Many teams already have critical business rules, but they are spread across files, notebooks, ETL logic, support documents, and ad hoc queries. That creates drift, weak governance, and slow operational changes.

MDM Lite gives those teams one governed place to manage the rules without pretending to be a full enterprise MDM platform.

## What The Product Delivers

1. one operational UI to manage mappings, groups, and parameters
2. approval and audit for lightweight governance
3. PostgreSQL-backed persistence in a customer-controlled environment
4. active SQL views for downstream technical consumption
5. a Windows-first trial baseline that can be installed and validated quickly

## What It Is Not

MDM Lite is not positioned today as:

1. enterprise MDM
2. a multi-tenant SaaS platform
3. a full catalog or lineage solution
4. a one-click packaged enterprise installer

## Ideal First Trial

The best first trial is with a small data or analytics team that already has rule-management pain and wants a governed alternative to spreadsheets or hardcoded mappings.

## Trial Message

The simplest message is:

MDM Lite gives a team one governed place to maintain small but critical business rules that are otherwise duplicated across SQL, notebooks, ETL, and support files.
